How it works

What am I actually paying for?

Customs duty — 10% of the car's value for cars from Great Britain. Cars from Northern Ireland are exempt because NI stays inside the EU customs union.

Import VAT — 23% charged on all cars imported from Great Britain, regardless of age. NI cars are exempt with the right proof. A VAT-registered dealer can reclaim it.

VRT (Vehicle Registration Tax) — charged once, when you register the car in Ireland, based on the car's OMSP (open market value) and CO₂ emissions. EVs get up to €5,000 relief.

NOx levy — a charge based on nitrogen-oxide emissions (diesel cars especially).

NCT — the Irish equivalent of an MOT; a first test is normally needed.

Registration fee — Revenue's charge to register the car.

Why does Northern Ireland make such a difference?

After Brexit, NI remains aligned with the EU for customs and VAT. A car already registered in NI moves to Ireland with no customs duty and no import VAT. The same car registered in Great Britain pays 10% duty and 23% VAT. That can easily save €5,000+ on a typical car.

Is this a guaranteed price?

No. This is a planning estimate. Revenue sets the final VRT using its own OMSP valuation, which can differ from the UK price. Always confirm with a customs agent or Revenue before committing.
